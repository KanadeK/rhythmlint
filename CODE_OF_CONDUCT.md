# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RhythmLint harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Protect calendar privacy: use synthetic or redacted fixtures in public reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, publication of private calendar data,
threats, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This concise policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles.
